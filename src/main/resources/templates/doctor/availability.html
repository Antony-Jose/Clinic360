About Time Slots

Your availability schedule is divided into 15-minute appointment slots. For example, if you set your availability from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, the system will create the following slots:

  • 9:00 AM - 9:15 AM
  • 9:15 AM - 9:30 AM
  • 9:30 AM - 9:45 AM
  • ... and so on until 11:45 AM - 12:00 PM

Patients will be able to book appointments during these slots based on your availability.
