Important Information

Cancellation Policy

Appointments can be cancelled up to 24 hours before the scheduled time without any charges. Late cancellations may incur a fee.

Late Arrival

Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time. Late arrivals may result in shortened consultation time or rescheduling if necessary.

Insurance

Please bring your insurance card and a valid ID to your appointment. Contact our office beforehand to verify if your insurance is accepted.
